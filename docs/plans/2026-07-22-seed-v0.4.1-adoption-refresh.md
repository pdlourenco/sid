# Seed-adoption refresh: re-baseline the open series to disciplined-project-seed v0.4.1

**Date:** 2026-07-22
**Scope:** epic #117 — the open stacked adoption PRs #125–#130 and #132, versus the
seed's current state.
**Seed baselines:** series authored against `18b10d1` (2026-06-04); current seed
`main` is **v0.4.1** (`49860ad`, 2026-07-22).
**Status:** executed (committed as a historical record on 2026-07-26). All refreshed
adoption PRs (#125, #126, #128, #129, #130, #132) merged; #131 landed; the `Verified by:`
member (#113/#127) was re-derived post-remediation and closed. This document is kept for
the decision trail (the per-release seed-sync discipline in it remains the standing guidance).

The open adoption series was cut seven weeks ago. Since then the seed released
v0.4.0 (2026-07-21) and v0.4.1 (2026-07-22): ~45 commits, +2,319/−96 across 42
files, five new ADRs (0010–0015). This plan re-baselines the adoption to v0.4.1
instead of merging the June snapshot.

Context worth knowing: the seed's v0.4.0 wave includes a four-adopter backport
study in which **SID is the fourth adopter** — the stacked-PR retrofit mechanics
of #125–#130 are now codified in the seed's README as the "governance retrofit"
profile, and at least one v0.4.0 convention (contract-artifact hardening) was
independently converged on by SID's issue #145. The two repos have been learning
from each other; the marker convention below is what keeps that loop explicit.

## 3. The `Verified by:` series member (#113 / PR #127)

Held out of the merge order, for two reasons:

- **Staleness:** its annotations predate the repo-wide review
  (`docs/analyses/2026-07-12-repo-wide-review.md`), which demonstrated the
  exact failure mode the seed now warns about — `Verified by:` claims that
  overstate what tests actually pin (~40 test-gap findings; gap issues
  #119–#124).
- **Conflict:** remediation Phase 3 (#143, #142, #137) edits `spec/SPEC.md`
  section-by-section.

Re-derive after remediation Phase 3 merges: re-annotate against the
then-current test suites (Phases 1–3 add the var-len, degenerate-excitation,
Welch cross-check, and uncertainty tests), adopt a closed verifier vocabulary
(`cross-vector` / `unit(M|Py)` / `lint` / `manual` / `deferred` / `none`), and
cross-reference the surviving gap issues. PR #127 can be recycled or reopened
fresh; either way #113 stays open until then.

## 5. Sync discipline going forward

On each seed release: read `meta/CHANGELOG.md` between the pinned and new
version, take/skip each entry with a one-line reason in the marker's sync log,
bump the pin. One small PR per release, no more seven-week archaeology.
